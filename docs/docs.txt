Reseny Connect: Website Workflow & Brand SOP
Reseny Business Mercantech, an initiative by Astransys Group | Parent: Astransys Global Pvt Ltd (IT company since 2017) | Website: www.resenyconnect.com
1. Executive Summary
Reseny Connect is an agentic-AI calling platform that calls leads automatically, qualifies them, follows up, and books appointments. Our competitors win on one axis: enterprise breadth, India-first voice and developer credibility, telephony scale, Self-serve, prompt-to-agent onboarding. None of them leads with the single outcome a business owner actually buys: a booked appointment on the calendar.
The recommended strategy:
•	Position Reseny as "lead-to-appointment autopilot", not as a voice-AI toolkit. Outcome first, technology second.
•	Make the website itself the demo. A visitor enters their number and, within seconds, receives a call from a Reseny AI agent that qualifies them and books their demo slot. Your product sells itself on your own homepage.
•	Serve both ends of the market: simple, self-serve, owner-friendly pages for small businesses, and trust, compliance and integration pages for mid-market and enterprise.
•	Build a compliance and trust layer (India telecom and data-privacy readiness, data handling, human handoff) as a visible selling point, because buyers are increasingly asking about it.
•	Use the Astransys heritage (IT company since 2017) as a credibility anchor that pure start-up competitors cannot copy.
  2. Why We are Different from our Competitors:
•	Every one of them has a prominent demo or trial call to action above the fold.
•	The strongest ones let the visitor experience the product instantly.
•	Industry pages and use-case pages are universal; so are integration pages, pricing, case studies and a blog.
•	Comparison and "alternatives" content is a proven search-traffic engine.
•	Nobody combines instant live demo, outcome-first messaging, owner-friendly pricing and visible compliance in one coherent experience. That is Reseny's gap.

6. The Master Visitor Journey and Conversion Workflow
This is the core workflow that connects the website to your AI tool.
6.1 Journey Stages
1.	Attract: Google (use-case and industry searches), LinkedIn, YouTube demos, WhatsApp shares, partner referrals, comparison articles, paid ads.
2.	Land: visitor arrives on the home page, an industry page or a use-case page (each with its own relevant demo).
3.	Experience: visitor hears the AI in action (three choices: "Call me now", "Listen to a sample call", "Try the interactive demo").
4.	Evaluate: visitor checks how it works, integrations, pricing, trust and compliance, case studies and comparisons.
5.	Convert: visitor books a demo or appointment, starts a pilot, or requests a quote.
6.	Nurture: automatic confirmation, reminders, and follow-up calls from Reseny itself.
7.	Onboard and grow: pilot, go-live, optimisation, referral.
6.2 Backend Workflow (Your Product Selling Your Product)
1.	Visitor submits the form (name, phone, business type, monthly lead volume, preferred language, preferred time).
2.	The lead is stored in your CRM with its source page and campaign tags.
3.	Reseny AI calls the visitor within seconds if they chose "Call me now," or at the chosen time.
4.	The AI confirms interest, asks three or four qualifying questions (business type, current lead handling, volume, timeline), and scores the lead.
5.	Hot lead: the AI offers available demo slots from your live calendar and books one. A confirmation goes out on WhatsApp, SMS and email with the meeting link.
6.	Warm lead: the AI sends a case study and pricing sheet, then schedules a follow-up call.
7.	Cold or not interested: tagged, added to a slow nurture list, and respected as do-not-call if requested.
8.	Reminders: a message the day before and a call or message an hour before the demo.
9.	No-show recovery: an automatic same-day call and WhatsApp offering a new slot.
10.	Post-demo: the sales team logs the outcome, the AI follows up on the proposal, and objections are handled.
11.	Internal alert: your sales team gets a summary (transcript, score, recording link) for every hot lead.
This journey doubles as your best case study: "Reseny uses Reseny."

8. Animation and Experience Specification
The site should feel alive, but fast. Animation must explain the product, not decorate it.
•	Hero animation: a phone shows a call in progress; speech bubbles appear; a calendar tile flips to "Booked." Loops softly.
•	Live voice waveform: a pulsing waveform that reacts when a sample call plays.
•	Scroll storytelling: the "how it works" section pins in place and advances step by step as the visitor scrolls.
•	Animated counters: numbers count up when they enter the screen (only real figures).
•	Lead-to-appointment flow diagram: nodes light up in sequence (Lead, Call, Qualify, Follow-up, Booked).
•	Industry cards: subtle hover lift, icon animation, and a preview of that sector's sample call.
•	Micro-interactions: buttons with soft glow, form fields with progress indicators, smooth page transitions.
•	Dark and light themes with a clean, premium look.
•	Performance rules: target fast loading on mobile networks; lazy-load videos and heavy animations; compress images; respect the "reduce motion" setting; keep layouts accessible (contrast, keyboard use, alt text).
•	Suggested stack (examples, not requirements): Next.js with Tailwind, Framer Motion or GSAP for animation, Lottie for icon animations, a headless CMS for the blog, and a CDN for speed.
